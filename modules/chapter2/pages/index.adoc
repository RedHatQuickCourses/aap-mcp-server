== The Problem We're Solving

=== Current Situation (Without MCP)

When a playbook fails, teams typically:

. Get alerted to the error.
. Search for a solution.
. Diagnose the problem.
. Develop and test a fix.
. Apply the fix to production.
. Verify the result.

This process is **manual, reactive, and time-consuming**, often taking 1–2 hours or longer.

=== New Situation (With MCP + Claude)

With MCP and Claude:

. The error is automatically captured with context.
. **Claude analyzes the error and suggests a solution.**
. **A human reviews and implements the suggested solution.**
. The playbook is rerun and the result is verified.

This approach makes troubleshooting **faster and more efficient while keeping the human in control of the fix.**
